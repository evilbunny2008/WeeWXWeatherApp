Isimo sezulu sakho, esivela esiteshini sakho sesimo sezulu.

Iningi lama-app esimo sezulu libonisa idatha evela kumasevisi angaphandle noma esiteshini esisemthethweni esingaba kude ngamakhilomitha amaningi. I-WeeWX Isimo Sezulu yona ibonisa izilinganiso ezivela esiteshini sakho somuntu siqu sesimo sezulu – noma yisiphi isiteshi onokufinyelela kuso – esisebenzisa i-WeeWX, isofthiwe yamahhala nevulekile yeziteshi zesimo sezulu.

IZICI
• Izimo zamanje ezivela esiteshini sakho, kuhlanganise izinga lokushisa, umswakama, umoya, imvula nomfutho womoya
• Izilinganiso zangaphakathi endlini (ozikhethela zona)
• Izibalo zanamuhla, izolo, zale nyanga, zenyanga edlule, zalo nyaka, zonyaka odlule nazo zonke izikhathi
• Izibikezelo ezivela kubahlinzeki abaningi, kuhlanganise i-BoM, NOAA (weather.gov), Met Office, DWD, AEMET, MET Norway, Met Éireann, MetService, Environment Canada ne-OpenWeatherMap
• Izithombe ze-radar yesimo sezulu nekhamera yewebhu ezingasondezwa ngeminwe
• Izibuyekezo ezicishe zibe ngesikhathi sangempela nge-MQTT ngesikhathi i-app ivuliwe
• Izaziso lapho izinga lokushisa, imvula yansuku zonke noma isivinini semvula kufinyelela imikhawulo oyibekile
• Iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya enemibala ekhanyayo, emnyama noma eyakho
• Imicimbi yenyanga ezayo
• Amayunithi e-metric noma e-imperial, kanye netimu ekhanyayo nemnyama
• Izikhathi zokubuyekeza eziguquguqukayo, nokukhetha ukubuyekeza nge-Wi-Fi kuphela
• Isebenza neziteshi ezisenethiwekhini yakho yasekhaya noma ku-inthanethi

IZIDINGO
Le app iyisengezo se-WeeWX futhi idinga iseva ye-WeeWX ezoxhumana nayo. Izandiso ze-WeeWX ezilandelayo kumele zifakwe kuleyo seva:
• InigoPlugin
• AggregateValues
• MQTTPublishV2 (ongazikhethela, iyadingeka ezibuyekezweni ezibukhoma)

Lezi zandiso zithumela idatha yakho yesimo sezulu ngefomethi i-app engayifunda. Imiyalelo yokusetha itholakala ku-wiki:
https://github.com/evilbunny2008/WeeWXWeatherApp/wiki
